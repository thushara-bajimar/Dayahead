<h2>1. The Product Plan:</h2>
<p>I am planning to build Dayahead, a web-based task planner and schedule management tool. It combines a user’s college or work timetable, daily tasks, homework/assignments, priorities, flexible deadlines (e.g., "complete before 7 PM"), and start/end times into a single platform. Users can also specify how many days in advance a task should appear before its deadline.</p>

<h2>2. Problem and The Solution:</h2>
<p>Currently, users track their obligations across fragmented tools such as WhatsApp messages, built-in phone to-do lists, and calendar apps like Google Calendar. Switching between multiple apps causes cognitive clutter and leads to forgotten deadlines.
Dayahead solves this by providing one centralized platform where users can see all their daily obligations in one place, minimizing forgotten tasks despite requiring initial task-entry effort from the user.</p>

<h2>3. Target Users:</h2>
<p>The primary target users are college students managing coursework, class schedules, and deadlines. However, the product is broadly designed for all types of users, including general students and working professionals who need structured daily schedule management.</p>

<h2>4. Main Use Case:</h2>
<p>The main use case follows a daily three-part routine:</p>
<ul>
    <li><b>Morning:</b> The user opens Dayahead to review the day's dashboard, check timetable events, review early-appearing upcoming tasks, and add any items missed the night before.</li>
    <li><b>During the day:</b> The user logs new tasks on the go and completes existing tasks in order of priority level and deadline constraints.</li>
    <li><b>Before bed:</b> The user updates task completion statuses, carries forward or snoozes unfinished tasks, and logs initial items for the following day.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Core Features:</h2>
<ul>
    <li><b>Daily Dashboard:</b> Combined view of daily tasks and timetable.</li>
    <li><b>Task Management:</b> Create, edit, and complete tasks with names, descriptions, and categories.</li>
    <li><b>Priority Levels:</b> High, Medium, and Low tags.</li>
    <li><b>Flexible Deadlines:</b> Target completion times (e.g., "before 7 PM") without needing rigid start times.</li>
    <li><b>Weekly Timetable:</b> Recurring schedule layout for classes or shifts.</li>
    <li><b>Task Status & Carry Forward:</b> Distinguish completed vs. pending tasks and rollover unfinished items.</li>
    <li><b>Filter & Sort:</b> Group/order tasks by date, priority, or category.</li>
    <li><b>Early Task Visibility:</b> Configure how many days prior to a deadline a task surfaces on the UI.</li>
    <li><b>Estimated Task Duration:</b> Track required effort in hours.</li>
    <li><b>Multi-Page Website:</b> Dedicated UI pages including Login and Registration layouts.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Assumptions About Users:</h2>
<ul>
    <li><b>Routine Engagement:</b> Users will open and check the platform at least three times a day (morning, midday, night).</li>
    <li><b>Manual Entry Willingness:</b> Users are willing to invest a small amount of daily time to input their tasks manually in exchange for organized tracking.</li>
    <li><b>Single-App Preference:</b> Users prefer a single consolidated tool over managing multiple separate productivity apps.</li>
    <li><b>Task Maintenance:</b> Users will actively manage unfinished tasks by snoozing or carrying them forward to future dates.</li>
    <li><b>Advance Planning:</b> Users know in advance how many days prior to a deadline they want to begin working on a specific task.</li>
</ul>

<h2>7. MVP includes:</h2>
<ul>
    <li><b>Daily Dashboard UI:</b> Centralized view of daily tasks and timetable.</li>
    <li><b>Task CRUD operations:</b> Add, Edit, Complete, Delete using local dummy data.</li>
    <li><b>Priority Levels:</b> High, Medium, and Low tags.</li>
    <li><b>Early Task Visibility:</b> Configure how many days prior to a deadline a task surfaces on the UI.</li>
    <li><b>Filter and Sort:</b> Group/order tasks by date, priority, or category.</li>
    <li><b>Front-End Authentication UI:</b> Login and Registration page demonstrations.</li>
    <li><b>Multi-Page Navigation:</b> Seamless transition between different sections of the application.</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. MVP doesn’t include:</h2>
<ul>
    <li><b>Backend & Database:</b> No server-side architecture or database persistence.</li>
    <li><b>Cloud Synchronization:</b> No cross-device syncing or saved user accounts.</li>
    <li><b>Push Notifications & Alarms:</b> No automated device alerts or reminders.</li>
    <li><b>External Integrations:</b> No automated email scraping or Google Calendar syncing.</li>
</ul>
